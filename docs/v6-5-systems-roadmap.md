# Immunostrat V6.5 systems roadmap

## Consolidated in V6.5

- Simple treatments: antibiotic, antiviral, anti-inflammatory.
- Campaign immune memory: known bacterial/viral profiles after completed missions.
- Simple pre-mission vaccination: optional antigen/cytokine preparation where configured.
- Local lymph exit: dendritic cells deliver debris information through a local lymphatic exit.
- Science vs gameplay notes: lightweight glossary entries for core systems.
- Campaign balance pass: mission 6 virus pressure reduced and treatment unlocks staged.

## Keep for V7

- Global body map.
- Regional lymph nodes.
- Organ-to-organ spread.
- Strategic movement between organs.

## Keep for V8-V10

- Infinite mode.
- Advanced threats: fungi, parasites, cancer cells.
- Playable pathogen factions.

## Keep for V10.5

- Final pre-polish systems audit.
- Performance pass for larger entity counts.
- More complete treatment balance.
- Better campaign objective diversity.
- UI grouping and tooltips.

## Keep for V11

- Visual polish only.
- Pixel art or final art direction.
- Advanced animations: phagocytosis, pseudopodes, antibodies, virus entry.
